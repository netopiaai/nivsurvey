Netopia AI Consumer Survey

Hi! Thank you for taking the time to answer Netopia's image advertisement survey! 😊

We are Netopia AI, a company focused on creating science-based solutions for the understanding of Filipino behavior and consumption.

In this survey, we will be asking you about your preferences and how you generally think and behave, as well as your thoughts and impressions of image advertisements. Your responses will help us understand how different people perceive and respond to advertising, and will be very valuable to our efforts in improving our capabilities and technology. All information that you will share will only be used:

  • to confirm your qualification to the survey
  • to inform our research about consumers’ preferences and improve our products based on those insights, and
  • to contact you for the incentive, or for future surveys.

Rest assured that all your responses will be anonymized and kept confidential, handled according to the Philippine’s data privacy laws. Only Netopia AI will have access to the original raw data. You may request access to your data at any time, and ask for it to be corrected, transferred or deleted. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at survey@netopia.ai.

The survey should take approximately 30 to 45 minutes to complete. There are no right or wrong answers, so feel free to answer as honestly as you can.

When you’re ready to take the survey, and have understood what the survey is for and know your rights as a respondent, click Start Survey to begin.
